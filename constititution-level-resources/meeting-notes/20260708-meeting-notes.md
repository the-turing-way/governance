- **AI use policy**
  - An active discussion in the community #4503
  - Draft policy points have been written outlining principles, how we can use generative AI, how to declare use of generative AI
  - Template declaration for use of generative AI in contributions
  - Are we promoting the use of AI?
    - Tricky position to take
    - Generally, we prefer human-written contributions
    - It isn't just about the output! We also want to teach people to write and give a space for people's writing.
    - However, taking an exclusionary position could turn people away from the community. Could worsen accessibility
    - Goal could be to define what acceptable use is, while ensuring everyone feels welcome however they do, or don't, use generative AI
  - JupyterHub's discussion: [https://github.com/jupyterhub/team-compass/pull/880](https://github.com/jupyterhub/team-compass/pull/880)
    - Final policy: [https://compass.hub.jupyter.org/contribute/llm/](https://compass.hub.jupyter.org/contribute/llm/)
  - We should also take a position on AI, LLMs and their usage. Not seek to detatch ourselves from the wider context and issues
    - Low-effort and harassment is not acceptable
    - There are environmental problems with AI (water, electricity)
    - Financial bubble?
    - Save small fixes and good-first-issues for people and first-time contributors
