# The Turing Way Steering Committee Role Voting Form

In this form, The Turing Way Steering Committee members will vote for their preferred candidates standing for the roles of Chair, Chair-Elect, and Secretary. We don't have a candidate for Secretary-Elect. The second highest voted candidate for Secretary role will be given a choice to take on this role.

**External support:** To ensure that this voting process is run as fairly as possible, [external person to be confirmed], with no involvement in The Turing Way's current Steering Committee work will be handling the voting data. 
They will be announcing the outcome to the Steering Committee on 18 June.
Below, you will find statements from candidates to help you make an informed decision about your vote.  
Please review the nominee information provided below before making your selections.

Since we are a small group, all members in the Steering Committee are required to participate in this voting process. 
In addition to voting for candidates, options for abstaining to vote, or voting for Re-Opening Nomination (AKA RON) has been provided.

A community-wide announcement will be made at the Community Forum on 25 June 2026.
